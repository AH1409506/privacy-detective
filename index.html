02 / FIELD NOTES

Know the rules of the digital world

Ethics and law overlap, but they are not the same thing.

ETHICS

What should we do?

Ethics considers fairness, honesty, consent, harm, and respect for people. Something can be technically legal but still be unfair or deceptive.

Example: An app should explain clearly why it needs sensitive information instead of surprising users later.

LAW · UNITED STATES

COPPA

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act applies to covered websites and online services directed to children under 13, and other covered services that have actual knowledge they collect personal information from children under 13.

Its requirements include notices and, in covered circumstances, verifiable parental consent, along with protections for children's personal information.

Read the FTC's official COPPA guidance ↗
PRIVACY PRACTICE

Reduce your digital trail

  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Share only the information that is necessary.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and multifactor authentication.
  • Check privacy settings and read important notices.
CONFIDENTIALITY

What is an NDA?

A non-disclosure agreement is a contract that sets rules for protecting and not improperly disclosing specified confidential information.

For example, a software developer may be required to keep unreleased product designs confidential. An NDA does not automatically make every secret lawful or override every other legal obligation.

Detective's rule: Collecting data can create risks even when a company has a legitimate purpose. Ask what is collected, why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is kept, and whether people have meaningful choices.
